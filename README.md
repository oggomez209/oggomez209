# Hey, I'm Marshall

Technically my name is Anthony. Nobody calls me that except HR, my email address, and people who are about to tell me something went wrong.

I'm a digital marketer who ended up writing code because the CMS wouldn't do what I needed and I'm too stubborn to accept "that's just how the template works."

## The day job

I'm a **Digital Marketing Manager** at a mission-driven CDFI credit union. I own the website, the ads, the email automation, and the analytics, which means when something breaks, there is a very short list of suspects and I'm on all of it.

On a normal week that looks like:

- Building landing pages inside an enterprise CMS that strips out my JavaScript like it's personally offended by it
- Writing schema markup so search engines, and now the robots that answer questions for people, actually understand what a page is about
- Building Looker Studio dashboards so other teams can see their own numbers without asking me first
- Running Google and LinkedIn campaigns, then explaining why the landing page matters more than the ad
- Hunting down the one line of CSS that made the entire site header vanish (it's always one line, and it's always `!important`)

## The side quest

**[Dymira Solutions](https://dymirasolutions.com)** is a consulting practice for organizations whose growth plans keep running into their own websites, data, and day-to-day operations. We work on both sides of that problem: getting found and getting chosen, then making sure the experience on the other side doesn't undo all of it.

I lead the **Digital Growth** side, which means:

- Websites and landing pages built to convert, not just to exist
- SEO and GEO so you show up in search results and in AI-generated answers
- Analytics that answer real questions instead of producing very colorful confusion
- Paid campaigns and marketing automation that actually connect to each other

Basically, I help businesses stop guessing. Guessing is fine for trivia night. It's an expensive way to run a marketing budget.

## What I'm into right now

- **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization):** SEO's younger sibling who showed up uninvited and is now running the house. Getting content cited by AI answer engines, not just ranked.
- **CMS migrations:** currently preparing one. Redirect maps are my love language. Broken links are my horror genre.
- **Responsible AI in marketing:** using it well, documenting how it's used, and being honest when it shouldn't be used at all.
